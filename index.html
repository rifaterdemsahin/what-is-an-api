What is an API?

A visual explanation of how software communicates using requests and responses.

1. The Restaurant Analogy

You (the Client) look at a menu and give an order to the Waiter (the API). The waiter takes your request to the Kitchen (the Server) and brings back your food (the Response).

2. Request & Response

Under the hood, your app sends a structured HTTP request and receives structured data (usually JSON) back:

GET /v1/weather?city=London
→ 200 OK {"temp": "18°C", "condition": "Sunny"}

3. Used Everywhere

Weather apps, Google Maps embeds, Stripe/PayPal checkouts, and "Sign in with Google" all rely on APIs to exchange data safely without exposing internal databases.
